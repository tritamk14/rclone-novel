Privacy policy

rclone-novel is used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive from the owner's own server.

  • No data is collected, stored, sold or shared with any third party.
  • Google Drive data is accessed only to copy the owner's files between the owner's server and the owner's Drive.
  • OAuth tokens stay on the owner's server and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
